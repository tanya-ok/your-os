# Who am I, and where do I fit after AI?

A fictional exploration with a sense of humour: from Senior IT specialist of everything to a person who can identify three trees and keep one tomato alive.

## Possible futures

- Gardener: the tomatoes still refuse to follow the roadmap.
- Forest steward: excellent uptime, occasional squirrels.
- Human: no model ID required.

## A small experiment

Spend an afternoon outside. Ask a gardener or forest steward what an ordinary working day looks like. Notice what feels interesting before designing a whole new life.

## Do not forget

AI can generate a forest. It cannot take my walk for me.
