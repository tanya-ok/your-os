# Gardening for beginners

This fictional example is a career experiment, not the owner's employment history.

From **Senior IT specialist of everything** to beginner gardener, with forest stewardship as a second path. The tomato plant does not care about a seniority title. It does care whether somebody noticed the soil.

## Overview

Keep systems thinking, observation and clear field notes. Learn to work with seasons, living things and uncertainty. Explore gardening first; compare it with forest stewardship through small, supervised practice.

## Timeline

1. Observe: choose a small growing space and keep a field notebook.
2. Practice: sow one suitable plant and follow its care needs.
3. Contribute: plan a small shared garden task and ask an experienced grower for feedback.
4. Compare: describe a day caring for a garden and a day tending a woodland path.

## Skills

- Soil observation and plant care
- Seasonal planning and patient follow-through
- Safe, supervised use of basic tools
- Habitat observation and careful field notes
