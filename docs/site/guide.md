# The twelve pages

The numbered menu follows three groups: **Movements**, **Home** and **Garden**, followed by **AI Agent**. Each page has its own job. Graph, Projects, Review and Setup are additional workspace tools.

## Movements

### 01 · Main

Return to the current day and week. Rhythm is a moving visual day ribbon with labelled fictional anchors, alongside manual Energy and Mood. It is an orientation aid, not a health assessment or prediction.

The main workspace connects weekly priority, decisions, context and projects with a personal garden of interests. Add or rename an interest, record minutes of practice and keep a brief note. Manual habits and support choices help you state what you want from the day. The lighthouse offers an optional light, sound and external camera; media starts only through its controls.

### 02 · Today

See the day’s tasks, cached calendar events and news. Feed management lives in Setup. Time-block planning lives in Work.

### 03 · GTD

Use Next, Waiting and Someday to distinguish an action you can take from a dependency or an idea. Capture into the bounded Markdown task list and preview supported writes. Optional Apple Reminders connection is configured in Setup and supplies a read-only local snapshot; it is separate from Markdown task editing.

### 04 · Calendar

Browse a Monday-to-Sunday week. Add, edit and delete local events. External subscription and native Apple events are read-only; a local event does not write back to Google or Apple. Manage connections in Setup and explicitly refresh when you need newer external data.

### 05 · Work

Plan work in time blocks: select a block, mark it complete or add another intention and start time. Once you change them, the day's blocks are saved as a Markdown record; they are not Google Calendar events. **Start** and **End** record a work session independently of the focus timer. Add completed work to the compact **Done today** list and browse earlier dates. **Meeting preparation** gathers yesterday/today completions, current area, today’s plan and **Do not forget**. GTD lists live on GTD. The log and sessions persist locally, including across days. The example does not require a company or project name.

## Home

### 06 · Home

Follow programme weeks, their themes, daily focus, tracker and review. Change the programme settings in Setup. This is a sequence measured from your chosen start, not the ISO week used by Career and Learning. Weekly context should remain visible when you move between days.

### 07 · Health

Use manual, descriptive records. Sleep records the night interval and common difficulties: date, bedtime, wake time, restfulness, difficulty falling asleep, night awakenings, difficulty waking up and nightmares. Add or edit a night, browse history, or undo a deletion. The calculated interval is time between the entered clocks, not measured sleep. These are manual observations; the examples provide no diagnosis or inferred condition.

### 08 · Library

Add books, audiobooks, videos, films, courses or articles, with a title or public HTTPS link. Adding a link or first opening its resource card looks up available public metadata; **Refresh resource details** retries explicitly. Source genres and categories become editable tags and filters. Your corrections and personal notes are preserved when metadata is refreshed. Resources, progress, genres and notes are saved in `Records/Library/resources.md`. A source may omit details, block automated access or exceed the bounded lookup size; manual editing remains available. No model is used for metadata or genre guesses, and personal notes are never sent in the lookup.

The public sample is **A Dictionary of Logic**, by **Thomas Macaulay Ferguson and Graham Priest**, published by **Oxford University Press in 2016**. Its bibliographic reference is real; the demo's reading status is fictional.

## Garden

### 09 · Career

Six distinct views keep the transition concrete: **Week**, **Overview**, **Timeline**, **Skills**, **Profile** and **Study Log**. “Gardening for beginners” explores garden care and forest stewardship through observation, skill assessment and practice. It makes no claim about real vacancies, qualifications or certification.

The joke has a practical limit: being a Senior IT specialist of everything does not let you `kubectl apply spring`. Choose a direction, check a small action, assess a skill, record practice and use the editable project note to connect the work.

### 10 · Learning

“How to be human” is an invented project with modules, an ISO-week rotation, learning sessions and resources. Choose a module, do a small exercise and record a reflection. A log should show what changed in your understanding, not merely count opened tabs.

### 11 · Future

Keep a longer horizon and possible directions without turning each one into a current obligation. Bring one idea into a project when you are ready to give it a next action. Horizon choices and notes are saved as a Markdown record; use an actual project for commitments with next actions.

### 12 · AI Agent

Select notes from explicitly configured vaults, prepare context, inspect source and line references, then choose whether to send. Local search does not call a model. An answer does not execute tools or edit notes. Conversations remain temporary; export a session before closing the page if you want to keep it. [Your AI agent](agent.md) explains the consent flow.

## Workspace tools

**Projects** owns editable project notes and archival. **Graph** visualizes supported relationships. **Review** gathers a weekly reflection with previewed Markdown writes, and its **Observations** section is where you review candidate observations for the model vaults. **Setup** owns preferences, vault roles and all connection management. Use quick capture with **⌘K / Ctrl+K**, page search with **⌘⇧K / Ctrl+Shift+K**, or **?** for available shortcut help outside a text field.
