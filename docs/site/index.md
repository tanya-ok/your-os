## A real workflow, with fictional details

The demo keeps the logic of a lived-in dashboard: work sessions, weekly themes, skill practice, reading, sleep and projects. Its connected examples are invented. **Gardening for beginners** follows a “Senior IT specialist of everything” learning to look after a growing space. **How to be human** makes room for observation, conversation and reflection.

You can change the projects, names and areas. The examples show how the system behaves; they do not define who should use it.

## Read the boundaries as carefully as the features

This is a standalone local application for Obsidian-compatible Markdown. It is not an Obsidian plugin. Your records are Markdown files; local SQLite holds only search and caches. AI sends, feed refreshes and native Apple access require explicit actions. [Scope and verification](status.md) explains the limits, including how this edition differs from the [original architecture article](https://tanya.engineer/blog/personal-os-architecture/).
